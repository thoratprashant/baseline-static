About Baseline Health

“There has to be a better way to serve this population.” Our founding physician, a Board-Certified Psychiatrist

How we started

Baseline Health was created by a Board-Certified Psychiatrist who often saw patients with stable symptoms and no need for medication changes for months, years or even decades.

Yet these patients still had to return regularly for time-consuming, face-to-face follow-up appointments to check in and obtain their refills.

Shortly after that realization, Baseline Health was created.

Our mission

To offer fast, convenient and cost-effective asynchronous telepsychiatry, so patients can obtain their mental health medication refills.

Who we serve

People with stable mental health symptoms, which we define as those who haven’t required any changes to their prescriptions for at least 3 months.

  • Care by web and text

    You interact with prescribers online, which makes vital care easy to reach.

  • Only what’s pertinent

    No expensive, time-consuming face-to-face appointments that often involve more small talk than exchange of pertinent information.

  • With or without insurance

    Especially helpful if you have no insurance, or prefer not to use it for your mental health medication.
