Cost and time

If you’re under-insured or have no insurance, the co-pay for seeing your prescriber regularly can amount to hundreds of dollars per visit, and most psychiatrists want to see you every 3 to 6 months (2 to 4 visits per year), even when nothing about your medication is changing.

Baseline Health charges a fraction of those co-pay fees and puts the power to decide how often you receive refills in your hands. Factor in the cost of travel (gas, car wear and tear, time off work and personal time) and the savings can be significant.

Beyond the savings above, Baseline Health offers separation from your employer and insurer, a more private way to obtain your medications in an age when companies may collect and sell your data.

Some people prefer this anonymity because they are community leaders, public figures or healthcare workers and aren’t comfortable with the traditional pathway to obtaining their mental health medications.

You skip regular in-person appointments with a psychiatrist, family physician, internist or nurse practitioner. Requesting a refill takes minutes, wherever you are, instead of pausing your life to travel to appointments or find a private place for a video visit.

Eligibility and care

No. At this time Baseline Health only treats stable patients who have been on the same medication(s) for 3+ months without changes.

If your medication needs to change (medication management), you’ll need to return to a more traditional form of care. You’re welcome back once you’ve been stable for 3+ months on the new dose or medication, as long as we offer it.

If you haven’t been on the same medication (dose, frequency and formulary) for at least 3 months, you’re more likely to need further adjustments to get stable. Because we don’t currently offer medication management, waiting at least 3 months is the minimum we recommend, to save you time and money.

Managing medications requires much more prescriber time, which would raise costs for our members. We hope to add medication management in the future, but today it isn’t compatible with our goal of offering refills at a low price.
