Refills without the appointments.

Baseline Health refills medication for stable patients: people who haven’t needed a change to their medication for at least 3 months. Verification is required.

What we can and can’t do for you

Becoming a patient

01of 06

Complete the initial assessment

  1. Complete the initial assessment

    5 to 15 minutes

    It covers all the pertinent information an in-person assessment with a psychiatrist or nurse practitioner would, in a much more succinct way. How long it takes depends on how extensive your mental health history is.

  2. Add your medications

    • We offer 17 medications to treat a number of psychiatric diagnoses.
    • If we offer what you need, add each medication to your profile: name, dose, formulary and frequency.
    • Choose your preferred pharmacy.

  3. Upload your proof of stability

    Stable means no changes to the medication dose, frequency or formulary for at least 3 months. We accept:

    • Recent medical records
    • A letter on practice letterhead
    • A pharmacy printout or screenshot

    Documents must be dated within 1 year of your refill request. You must also certify that you haven’t been off your medication for more than 1 week, because restarting at a higher dose after a longer gap can cause adverse side effects.

  5. Make a payment

    Your payment includes the initial assessment fee and the membership fee.

  6. Submit and wait to hear from your prescriber

    3 to 5 business days
    • Once your payment is processed, your assessment, refill requests and supporting documents go to your prescriber.
    • If they have questions or need more documents, you’ll get a notification.
    • Once everything is resolved, your prescription is sent to your preferred pharmacy.

Already a member?

  • Log in to your account and click the button to request refills.
  • If your medication hasn’t changed since we last prescribed it, the refill is sent to your pharmacy as soon as your membership fee is processed.
  • If you’re requesting a change to a medication we prescribed, you’ll upload documents showing you’ve been stable on the new dose, frequency or formulary for at least 3 months.
  • If a prescriber outside Baseline Health has started you on a new medication that we also offer, add it to your profile.
  • Upload documents showing you’ve been stable on it for at least 3 months, and pay a new medication review fee.
  • Once the request is processed, the medication is sent to your preferred pharmacy within 3 to 5 business days.

The initial assessment fee and the new medication review fee are non-refundable, because they pay the prescriber for the time spent reviewing your information.

If the prescriber can’t approve your initial medication request for any reason, your membership fee is refunded.
