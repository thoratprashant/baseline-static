How it works

This is a quick guide, not a medical assessment. A prescriber reviews every request, and proof of stability is required.

Stable means no changes to your medication, dose, frequency or formulary for at least 3 months.

What we do: medication refills

You keep taking what already works. We renew your current prescription and send it to the pharmacy you choose, through web and text-based contact with a prescriber.

What we don’t do yet: medication management

Dose increases, switches and new medications need a prescriber outside Baseline Health. Once you’ve been stable again for 3 months, you’re welcome back.

Three things to do. Then your prescriber takes over.

  1. Complete the assessment

    An online questionnaire that covers what an in-person visit would, in about 5 to 15 minutes.

  2. Add your meds and proof

    List what you take, choose your pharmacy and upload records, a prescriber letter or a pharmacy printout.

  3. Choose a membership

    Pay, submit and allow 3 to 5 business days for your prescriber to review and send your refill.
